More company, more care: **!StayAlone** 1.2.0.

## What's new

- **Water goal** — set how many glasses a day (8 by default) and log each one from the panel, which now shows a progress bar. The mascot celebrates when you hit the goal, counts your streak of days, and on Monday mornings sums up the last week (time together, breaks and water). The history stays in `state.ini`, on your PC.
- **Guided breaks** — when the eyes or stretch reminder shows up, click it and the mascot walks you through the break, step by step, with a countdown.
- **Focus your way** — choose the focus and break minutes of the pomodoro, and let reminders wait for the break instead of interrupting you.
- **Ctrl+Alt+M** opens the chat from anywhere. Only that key combination is registered; the app never reads the keyboard. You can turn it off in *Settings → Chat*.
- **Seasonal hats** — a Santa hat at Christmas, a witch hat at Halloween, a straw hat in June and a party hat on your birthday and New Year's. Works with 16×16 and 32×32 mascots, and can be turned off.
- **The two mascots play together** — the buddy naps next to your mascot when it's sleeping, they greet each other and share the treat.
- **About page** — at the bottom of the Settings sidebar: what the app is, its version and release date, and links to my projects page and to the GitHub repository.
- Fresh screenshots in the README, and a UI smoke test (`tools/smoke.ps1`) that checks the app in an isolated data folder.

## Download

**[`dontStayAlone.exe`](https://github.com/4TyllaL/notStayAlone/releases/download/v1.2.0/dontStayAlone.exe)** — one file, the whole app (AI chat included). If you're on 1.1.x, the app offers this update by itself. Windows 10/11, 64-bit; no installer, settings live in `%APPDATA%\StayAlone` and are kept when you update.

> Why *dont*StayAlone? The app is **!StayAlone**, but GitHub strips the `!` from file names.

The binary is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn on first run (*More info → Run anyway*). GitHub shows its SHA-256 next to the download.
