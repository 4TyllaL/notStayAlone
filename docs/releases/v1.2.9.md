Your mascot feels your PC: **!StayAlone** 1.2.9.

## What changed

- **Restless when the PC is heavy:** when the CPU or memory stays maxed out for a while, your mascot sweats and paces back and forth, and cheers when the PC can breathe again. It only reads the PC's totals every 6 seconds (next to no CPU). Choose how easily it gets restless in the new *Settings → Performance* page, or turn it off.
- **Lighten the PC (optional, off by default):** turn it on in *Settings → Performance*, then give your mascot a treat (or click its "the PC is heavy" bubble) and it lists your 3 heaviest programs. You choose: **close** it, like clicking the X (the program can still ask you to save), or **make it lighter** (lower priority). Nothing is closed unless you pick it, and Windows programs, other users' programs and programs without a window never show up.
- **Plugin folders fixed:** a plugin allowed to read a folder can now read it when you press *Test* before saving (it used to get "access denied"). Closing *Settings* without saving takes that access away again.

## Download

**[`dontStayAlone.exe`](https://github.com/4TyllaL/notStayAlone/releases/download/v1.2.9/dontStayAlone.exe)** — one file, the whole app (AI chat included). If you're on 1.1.x or 1.2.x, the app offers this update by itself. Windows 10/11, 64-bit; no installer, settings live in `%APPDATA%\StayAlone` and are kept when you update.

`dontStayAlone.exe.sig` is the update signature; the app downloads and checks it by itself, so you don't need it. `BUILDINFO.txt` is for anyone who wants to audit or rebuild the release (build a clean checkout at `C:\StayAloneBuild\src` with the toolchain it lists and you get the same SHA-256).

> Why *dont*StayAlone? The app is **!StayAlone**, but GitHub strips the `!` from file names.

The binary is not code-signed (Authenticode) yet, so SmartScreen may warn on first run (*More info → Run anyway*). GitHub shows its SHA-256 next to the download.
