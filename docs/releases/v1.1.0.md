A big update to **!StayAlone**: it now speaks English, updates itself and brings a lot more company.

## What's new

- **English interface** — the whole app, the mascots' lines and the AI chat now come in English too. It follows your Windows language, or pick one in *Settings → General → Appearance*.
- **Automatic updates** — once a day it checks this repository for a new version; the panel offers it and the download is verified (SHA-256) before replacing the `.exe`. You can turn it off in Settings.
- **Welcome screen** — the first time you open it, pick your mascot, your reminders and (optionally) paste a Gemini key.
- **Log your water** — new *I drank a glass of water* row in the panel (and `--agua` for shortcuts), with today's count. The daily summary finally knows how much you drank.
- **A buddy on screen** — choose a second mascot that walks around, visits your main one and chases the ball too.
- **Memory** — the mascot can remember what you tell it in chat (an exam on Friday, your cat's name) and bring it up later. Stored only in a text file on your PC that you can view, edit or wipe; passwords, documents and numbers are never kept.
- **Routine and mood** — birthday wishes, Monday and Friday lines, a nudge after three hours without a break, and it stays quiet during meetings (Teams, Zoom, Webex…).
- **Community gallery** — install mascots and plugins shared in the repository straight from Settings. Every file is checked against its SHA-256. First items: *Calcifer HD*, *Pingo* the penguin and the *Frase do dia* plugin.
- **Better mascot maker** — mascots can be 32×32, and you can draw your own sleeping, eating, happy and walking poses (with the standing pose shown faintly underneath).
- **Dark mode** — follows Windows, or choose light or dark.

## Download

**[`dontStayAlone.exe`](https://github.com/4TyllaL/notStayAlone/releases/download/v1.1.0/dontStayAlone.exe)** — one file, the whole app (AI chat included). Download it and run it. Windows 10/11, 64-bit; no installer, settings live in `%APPDATA%\StayAlone` and are kept when you update.

> Why *dont*StayAlone? The app is **!StayAlone**, but GitHub strips the `!` from file names.

The binary is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn on first run (*More info → Run anyway*). GitHub shows its SHA-256 next to the download.
